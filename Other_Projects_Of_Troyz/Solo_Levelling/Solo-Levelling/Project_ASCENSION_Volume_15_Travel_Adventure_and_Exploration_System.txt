PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 15 – Travel, Adventure & Exploration SystemBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define how travel, exploration, cultural experiences, adventures, expeditions, and real-world discoveries integrate into the Life RPG. This system transforms journeys into meaningful progression while encouraging planning, safety, and personal growth.
1. Executive Summary
Overview of the Travel System and how exploration contributes to player progression.
2. Design Goals
Encourage meaningful travel, cultural learning, adventure, planning and safe exploration.
3. World Exploration Architecture
Countries, states, cities, landmarks, hidden locations, travel zones, exploration regions and discovery maps.
4. Travel Planning
Destination planning, itineraries, budgeting, packing lists, visas, transport, accommodation and weather preparation.
5. Exploration Progression
Exploration XP, explorer ranks, discovery milestones, region completion, travel streaks and legendary expeditions.
6. Adventure Quests
Pilgrimages, hiking, cycling, road trips, heritage visits, photography missions, food exploration and cultural experiences.
7. AI Travel Guide
Generate itineraries, recommend attractions, optimize routes, estimate budgets and adapt plans in real time.
8. Collectibles & Memories
Travel journal, photo albums, souvenirs, digital passport stamps, memory timeline and exploration badges.
9. Social Travel
Travel parties, guild expeditions, shared itineraries, safety check-ins and collaborative adventures.
10. Decision Rationale
Why exploration should reward learning and experience instead of distance travelled alone.
11. Edge Cases
Trip cancellation, flight delays, poor connectivity, lost devices, safety incidents, changing weather.
12. Example Scenarios
Weekend city trip, solo backpacking, temple pilgrimage, business travel, international vacation.
13. Implementation Notes
Maps integration, offline caching, optional location permissions, synchronization, battery optimization.
14. Risks
Privacy around location data, excessive battery use, inaccurate travel data, notification overload.
15. Future Enhancements
AR exploration, AI tour companion, smart-glasses support, collaborative world events and travel marketplace.
