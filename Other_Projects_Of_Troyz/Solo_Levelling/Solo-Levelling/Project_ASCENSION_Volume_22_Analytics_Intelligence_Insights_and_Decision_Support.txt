PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 22 – Analytics, Intelligence, Insights & Decision SupportBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define the analytics platform that transforms player activity into meaningful insights, predictions, dashboards and intelligent recommendations for users, AI agents and administrators.
1. Executive Summary
Overview of the analytics ecosystem and decision-support capabilities.
2. Design Goals
Deliver actionable insights while preserving privacy, accuracy and explainability.
3. Analytics Architecture
Event collection, data warehouse, streaming analytics, dashboards, reporting and AI insight generation.
4. Event Tracking
Gameplay events, health events, learning events, finance events, social events and custom telemetry.
5. Dashboard System
Player dashboard, AI dashboard, admin dashboard, developer dashboard and executive dashboard.
6. Intelligence Engine
Trend detection, anomaly detection, predictions, recommendations and behavioral insights.
7. KPI Framework
Engagement, progression, retention, wellbeing, learning, finance, performance and operational metrics.
8. Reporting
Daily, weekly, monthly, seasonal and lifetime reports with export capabilities.
9. AI Decision Support
Context summaries, predictive alerts, goal forecasting and adaptive recommendations.
10. Decision Rationale
Why analytics should focus on improving user outcomes instead of maximizing screen time.
11. Edge Cases
Missing data, duplicate events, delayed synchronization, conflicting metrics, privacy restrictions.
12. Example Scenarios
Study performance review, health trend analysis, financial progress, team analytics.
13. Implementation Notes
Telemetry pipeline, warehouse schema, ETL processes, visualization layer, observability.
14. Risks
Incorrect insights, data bias, excessive data collection, dashboard overload.
15. Future Enhancements
Digital twin analytics, predictive simulations, AI-generated executive reports, cross-platform benchmarking.
