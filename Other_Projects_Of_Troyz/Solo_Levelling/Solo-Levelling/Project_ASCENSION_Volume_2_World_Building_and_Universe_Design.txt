PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 2 – World Building & Universe DesignBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define the fictional universe and the mapping between the game world and real life. This foundation establishes the terminology, structure, and design direction for all later gameplay systems.
1. Executive Summary
Describe the game universe, the role of the Player, the AI System, and how real-life actions become events inside the world.
2. World Vision
Goals, emotional tone, inspirations, design pillars, and the intended player experience.
3. Universe Structure
Layers of the world (Reality Layer, System Layer, Story Layer, Social Layer, Future Layer).
4. Regions
Health Kingdom, Knowledge Kingdom, Finance Kingdom, Career Kingdom, Adventure Kingdom, Relationship Kingdom, Creativity Kingdom.
5. Factions & Guilds
Design faction identities, guild rules, progression, reputation, and cooperative mechanics.
6. Player Identity
Classes, subclasses, titles, ranks, reputation, alignment, prestige, rebirth.
7. NPC System
AI Mentor, Merchant, Blacksmith, Librarian, Trainer, Healer, Guild Master, Rival, Companion.
8. Story Engine
Seasonal story arcs, daily narrative generation, milestones, cutscene concepts.
9. World Events
Weather events, festivals, travel events, financial events, learning events, health events.
10. Decision Rationale
Why a unified world is better than isolated productivity modules. Alternatives considered and trade-offs.
11. Edge Cases
New users, long inactivity, failed quests, impossible goals, disabled permissions, travel across time zones.
12. Example Scenarios
Student, entrepreneur, athlete, traveler, and creator progressing through the world.
13. Implementation Notes
Modules required, backend services, AI dependencies, localization, scalability considerations.
14. Risks
Feature overload, narrative inconsistency, accessibility concerns, content moderation, privacy.
15. Future Expansion
Additional kingdoms, multiplayer regions, AR locations, community-created worlds.
