PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 4 – Progression System & Game EconomyBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define how players grow over time through experience, levels, rewards, currencies, unlocks, milestones, and balancing. This volume specifies the core progression loop that connects every other system.
1. Executive Summary
Overview of the progression philosophy and how actions convert into meaningful advancement.
2. Design Goals
Reward consistency, avoid manipulation, support long-term engagement, adapt to individual players.
3. Experience (XP) System
Sources of XP, hidden XP, combo bonuses, streak modifiers, diminishing returns, anti-exploit rules.
4. Leveling System
Player levels, account levels, class levels, milestone rewards, progression pacing.
5. Skill Points & Unlocks
Unlock skills, passive abilities, active abilities, specialization paths.
6. Currency System
Gold, Gems, Tokens, Reputation Points, Event Currency, Premium Cosmetics (optional).
7. Rewards
Daily rewards, quest rewards, achievement rewards, seasonal rewards, mystery rewards.
8. Prestige & Rebirth
Optional long-term reset mechanics with permanent benefits and exclusive content.
9. Milestones
Personal goals, life achievements, boss victories, legendary accomplishments.
10. Balancing Philosophy
Risk vs reward, fairness, avoiding pay-to-win, adaptive difficulty.
11. Decision Rationale
Why XP, levels and milestones are used; alternatives considered; trade-offs.
12. Edge Cases
Offline progress, cheating attempts, duplicate rewards, changing goals, migration between devices.
13. Example Progression
Example journey from Level 1 to Level 50 across study, fitness, finance and career.
14. Implementation Notes
Database entities, reward services, progression engine, event processing.
15. Risks & Future Enhancements
Inflation, reward fatigue, balancing complexity, future seasonal systems.
