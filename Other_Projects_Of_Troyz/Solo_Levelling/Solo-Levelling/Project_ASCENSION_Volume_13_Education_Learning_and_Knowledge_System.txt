PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 13 – Education, Learning & Knowledge SystemBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define the learning ecosystem that transforms education, skill acquisition, knowledge retention, certifications, and lifelong learning into core gameplay systems.
1. Executive Summary
Overview of the Education System and its integration with quests, skills and progression.
2. Design Goals
Encourage continuous learning, measurable progress, knowledge retention and practical application.
3. Learning Architecture
Courses, books, articles, videos, projects, certifications, notes and revision.
4. Study Management
Study plans, sessions, Pomodoro integration, focus tracking, exam preparation and scheduling.
5. Knowledge Progression
Learning XP, mastery levels, revision streaks, concept maps and milestone achievements.
6. AI Learning Coach
Personalized study plans, revision scheduling, weakness detection, quiz generation and recommendations.
7. Learning Quests
Daily study missions, project goals, certification challenges, reading quests and research objectives.
8. Knowledge Repository
Notes, bookmarks, summaries, flashcards, linked resources and searchable knowledge base.
9. Assessments & Certifications
Practice tests, mock exams, project reviews, portfolio tracking and external certifications.
10. Decision Rationale
Why learning should be based on mastery and consistency instead of time spent alone.
11. Edge Cases
Interrupted studies, changing subjects, failed exams, imported progress, offline learning.
12. Example Scenarios
University student, self-taught developer, entrepreneur learning finance, language learner.
13. Implementation Notes
Learning database, synchronization, AI recommendation engine, analytics, integrations.
14. Risks
Information overload, poor recommendation quality, excessive notifications.
15. Future Enhancements
Adaptive curricula, collaborative learning, institution integrations, AI tutors with multimodal support.
