PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 3 – Player System & Character ArchitectureBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define every aspect of the player, including identity, progression, attributes, customization, ranks, titles, classes, progression rules, and the technical foundation required to represent a player's journey.
1. Executive Summary
Overview of the Player System and its role as the core of the Life RPG.
2. Design Goals
Create a deeply personalized player identity that evolves with real-life progress.
3. Player Profile
Username, avatar, biography, goals, preferences, privacy settings, accessibility preferences.
4. Character Creation
Choose class, difficulty, life goals, interests, preferred coaching style, initial assessments.
5. Classes & Subclasses
Developer, Entrepreneur, Trader, Student, Athlete, Creator, Explorer, Scholar with specialization paths.
6. Attributes & Stats
Strength, Focus, Discipline, Creativity, Wealth, Health, Reputation, Energy, Wisdom, Leadership and configurable custom stats.
7. Progression System
Levels, XP, prestige, rebirth, milestones, passive bonuses, unlock conditions.
8. Titles & Achievements
Permanent titles, seasonal titles, hidden achievements, collectible badges.
9. Reputation & Alignment
Track trust, consistency, teamwork, reliability, and optional morality-style alignment for story interactions.
10. Customization
Themes, avatars, animations, UI layouts, AI voice, profile cosmetics.
11. Decision Rationale
Why RPG progression encourages engagement more effectively than static task lists; alternatives and trade-offs.
12. Edge Cases
Changing goals, deleting progress, inactive users, conflicting goals, multiple devices, accessibility requirements.
13. Example Player Journeys
Student, entrepreneur, traveler, fitness enthusiast, lifelong learner.
14. Implementation Notes
Core database entities, synchronization strategy, profile services, permissions, scalability.
15. Risks & Future Enhancements
Over-customization, balancing complexity, feature discoverability, future additions such as AI-generated classes.
