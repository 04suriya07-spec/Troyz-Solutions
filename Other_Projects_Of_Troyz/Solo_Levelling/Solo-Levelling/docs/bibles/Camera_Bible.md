# ASCENSION Camera Bible

ASCENSION is 2D, but it is directed like a film. The "Camera" is the user's focus. We do not just fade screens; we direct the eye through physical space.

## Core Principles
1. **Depth over Flatness:** UI elements exist in Z-space. The background is far; the Companion is near.
2. **Focus Pulling:** When something is important, the background blurs, simulating a lens aperture opening.
3. **Intentional Movement:** The camera only moves when the narrative demands a shift in perspective.

---

## The Camera Lexicon

### 1. The Push-In
**Visual:** The entire UI scales up slightly (1.05x). Background blur increases. The central element becomes perfectly sharp.
**Used For:**
- Mission Accepted
- Reading a Chronicle Entry
- Intimate Companion Dialogue

### 2. The Pull-Out
**Visual:** The camera steps back. The scale reduces to 0.95x, revealing the larger context.
**Used For:**
- The Mirror Moment (Revealing the Reality Graph)
- Transitioning from a single Memory to a Campaign view

### 3. The Pan
**Visual:** The UI slides smoothly along the X or Y axis, as if looking at a massive canvas.
**Used For:**
- Exploring the Journey
- Moving between the Morning Brief and Mission Control

### 4. The Macro (Book Zoom)
**Visual:** Extreme zoom into a single element (e.g., a book or a node), turning that element into the new full-screen background.
**Used For:**
- Chronicle opens -> Book Zoom -> Page Turn -> Narration
