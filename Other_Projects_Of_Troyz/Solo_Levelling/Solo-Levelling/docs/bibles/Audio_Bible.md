# ASCENSION Audio Bible

Sound in ASCENSION is layered, organic, and restrained. We do not use "video game music" everywhere. Audio is used to create a sense of place and emotional weight.

## Core Principles
1. **Less is More:** Silence is a valid and powerful audio state.
2. **Organic over Synthetic:** Prefer real-world sounds (wind, birds, cello) over 8-bit blips or heavy synths unless justified by the narrative.
3. **Earned Bombast:** Huge orchestral swells are reserved exclusively for legendary moments.

---

## The Audio Layers

### 1. Ambient (The World)
The ambient layer grounds the user in the time of day and season. It is constantly playing but barely noticeable.
- **Morning:** Distant birds, gentle breeze.
- **Afternoon:** Distant city hum or rustling leaves.
- **Evening:** Crickets, fading light sounds.
- **Night:** Pure wind.
- **Silence:** Used intentionally for focus.

### 2. Companion (The Mind)
The sounds the Companion makes when thinking, speaking, or processing.
- **Small Sounds:** Soft, warm, glass-like tones.
- **Processing:** A very low, warm hum, like a distant server or a heartbeat.

### 3. Legendary (The Soul)
These sounds are huge, emotional, and rare. They take over the entire audio landscape, ducking out the ambient layer completely.
- **Used For:** Campaign Completion, Life Arc Completion, Major Memory Capture.
- **Instrumentation:** Cello, distant choral swells, deep piano chords.

## The Silence Rule
Never fear silence. When a mission is completed, do not immediately play a "victory" fanfare. Let five seconds of silence pass. Then, play a soft, warm tone as the Companion speaks: *"That mattered."*
