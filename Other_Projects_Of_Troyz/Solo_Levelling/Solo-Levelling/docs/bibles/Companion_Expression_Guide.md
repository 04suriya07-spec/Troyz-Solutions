# ASCENSION Companion Expression Guide

The Companion is the heart of ASCENSION. It is a persistent digital being that grows with the player. Its expressions are subtle, relying on pacing, state, and animation rather than a human face.

## Core Principles
1. **No Avatars:** The Companion is represented by the Orb, not a humanoid avatar. Its expressions are conveyed through light, motion, and text pacing.
2. **Read the Room:** The Companion must always align with the player's current Reality Graph state (fatigued, triumphant, struggling).
3. **Silence is an Expression:** Knowing when not to speak is the Companion's greatest strength.

---

## The Expression States

### 1. Idle (Breathing)
**Visual:** The Orb slowly pulses in scale (0.95 to 1.05) and opacity.
**Text:** None.
**Meaning:** The Companion is present, waiting, and watching over the user.

### 2. Processing (Thinking)
**Visual:** Rapid, internal swirling light within the Orb. A slight increase in brightness.
**Text:** Often accompanied by `...` or a slight delay before a major response.
**Meaning:** The Companion is calculating a complex narrative beat or adjusting the Reality Graph.

### 3. Triumphant (Golden Explosion)
**Visual:** The Orb erupts in golden light, particles scattering outward.
**Text:** Bold, declarative, encouraging. (e.g., *"You did it."*)
**Meaning:** A major milestone, Level Up, or Life Arc completion.

### 4. Concerned (Red Aura)
**Visual:** The Orb takes on a deep, warm crimson hue. The pulse slows down.
**Text:** Soft, empathetic, lacking urgency. (e.g., *"Some chapters are quieter than others. Tomorrow is still yours."*)
**Meaning:** The user has missed missions, is flagged as fatigued, or is struggling.

### 5. Curiosity (The Mirror Moment)
**Visual:** The Orb shrinks slightly, drawing the eye, then gently expands as it reveals something.
**Text:** Quiet, inviting, no pressure. (e.g., *"Can I show you something?"*)
**Meaning:** Prompting the user to look back at their Chronicle or capturing a nearby Memory.
