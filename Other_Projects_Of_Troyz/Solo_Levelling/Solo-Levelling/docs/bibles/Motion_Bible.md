# ASCENSION Motion Bible

Every animation in ASCENSION has a name, a purpose, and an emotional weight. Motion is not decorative; it is the language through which the world communicates with the user.

## Core Principles
1. **Never Distract:** If an animation draws attention to itself rather than the emotion of the moment, it is wrong.
2. **Physics of the Mind:** Animations should feel like thoughts—fluid, weightless, but grounded in intention.
3. **The Pause:** Every major moment must pause time. Motion stops to give the achievement weight.

---

## The Motion Lexicon

### 1. Ascend
**Visual:** Elements gently float upward while fading in.
**Duration:** 800ms
**Easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`
**Used For:**
- Level Up
- Memory Created
- Chronicle Entry
- Mission Complete

### 2. Pulse
**Visual:** A subtle, warm expansion and contraction of scale and opacity.
**Duration:** 2000ms (infinite) or 600ms (single)
**Easing:** `ease-in-out`
**Used For:**
- Companion breathing (Idle)
- New Signals (Notifications)
- Mission Available

### 3. Collapse
**Visual:** An element shrinks inward, dissolving into a pinpoint of light or dust.
**Duration:** 400ms
**Easing:** `cubic-bezier(0.8, 0, 0.2, 1)`
**Used For:**
- Mission disappears (transitioning into a Memory)
- Closing the Chronicle

### 4. The Pause (Time Freeze)
**Visual:** The entire UI stops. Background particles freeze. The only moving element is the Companion Orb slowly turning to face the user.
**Duration:** 3000ms - 5000ms
**Used For:**
- Major Campaign Completion
- Life Arc Milestone
- The Mirror Moment
