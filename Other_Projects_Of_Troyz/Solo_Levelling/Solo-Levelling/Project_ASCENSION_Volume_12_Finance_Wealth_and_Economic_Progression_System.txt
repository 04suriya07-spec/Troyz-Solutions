PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 12 – Finance, Wealth & Economic Progression SystemBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define how real-world financial growth integrates with player progression. This volume covers budgeting, income, investing, net worth, business activities, financial quests, economic analytics, and wealth-oriented gameplay.
1. Executive Summary
Overview of the Finance System and its role in long-term player development.
2. Design Goals
Promote financial literacy, responsible planning, and sustainable wealth building without encouraging risky behavior.
3. Financial Architecture
Income, expenses, savings, budgets, investments, liabilities, assets, insurance, taxes (where applicable).
4. Wealth Progression
Financial XP, wealth levels, net-worth milestones, savings streaks, investment achievements.
5. Budgeting System
Category budgets, spending limits, alerts, monthly reviews, AI-assisted budgeting.
6. Investment & Business Tracking
Portfolio tracking, business goals, passive income milestones, entrepreneurship quests.
7. AI Finance Coach
Cash-flow analysis, saving suggestions, spending insights, financial goal planning.
8. Financial Quests
Emergency fund, debt reduction, investment learning, budgeting challenges, income growth missions.
9. Rewards & Recognition
Finance titles, badges, wealth achievements, reputation bonuses for consistent planning.
10. Decision Rationale
Why financial progression should focus on education and habits rather than speculation.
11. Edge Cases
Irregular income, shared finances, currency changes, missing data, offline tracking.
12. Example Scenarios
Student budget, salaried professional, entrepreneur, freelancer, investor.
13. Implementation Notes
Secure financial data storage, optional integrations, analytics engine, privacy controls.
14. Risks
Privacy, inaccurate imported data, over-reliance on recommendations, notification fatigue.
15. Future Enhancements
Bank integrations (where available), financial simulations, family budgeting, AI forecasting.
