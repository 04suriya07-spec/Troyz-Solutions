PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 7 – Skill System & Talent TreesBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define how players acquire, improve, specialize and master skills. This volume establishes the framework for talent trees, mastery, unlocks, cross-skill relationships, and lifelong progression.
1. Executive Summary
Overview of the Skill System and its relationship with quests, XP and progression.
2. Design Goals
Support lifelong learning, meaningful specialization, adaptability and measurable growth.
3. Skill Categories
Technical, Business, Finance, Health, Fitness, Creativity, Communication, Languages, Relationships, Lifestyle, Survival and Custom Skills.
4. Skill Trees
Branches, prerequisites, unlock paths, passive skills, active skills, hybrid skills and elite specializations.
5. Mastery System
Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert, Master, Grandmaster, Legendary progression.
6. Skill Experience
Skill XP sources, decay rules (optional), practice bonuses, consistency multipliers and milestone rewards.
7. Certifications & Proof
Link external certifications, completed projects, portfolios, badges and verified achievements.
8. Cross-Skill Synergy
How improving one skill influences related skills and unlocks combined abilities.
9. AI Learning Coach
Recommend learning paths, detect weaknesses, schedule practice and personalize study plans.
10. Decision Rationale
Why a modular skill system is preferred over simple counters; alternatives and trade-offs.
11. Edge Cases
Changing career goals, abandoned skills, duplicate evidence, imported achievements, offline learning.
12. Example Learning Paths
Developer, Entrepreneur, Trader, Student, Athlete and Creator journeys.
13. Implementation Notes
Skill database, dependency graphs, synchronization, analytics, recommendation engine.
14. Risks
Overly complex trees, progression imbalance, certification fraud, feature discoverability.
15. Future Enhancements
Community skill trees, AI-generated specializations, institution partnerships, marketplace integration.
