PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 10 – Social, Guilds & Multiplayer EcosystemBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define how players interact, collaborate, compete and build communities. This volume covers guilds, parties, friends, mentoring, leaderboards, cooperative quests, reputation, moderation and social progression.
1. Executive Summary
Overview of the social ecosystem and its role in long-term engagement.
2. Design Goals
Encourage healthy collaboration, accountability and shared achievements while avoiding toxic competition.
3. Social Architecture
Friends, followers, parties, guilds, clans, alliances and communities.
4. Guild System
Guild creation, roles, permissions, progression, shared resources, guild levels and guild achievements.
5. Multiplayer Activities
Co-op quests, guild raids, study groups, fitness teams, startup circles and travel squads.
6. Reputation & Trust
Trust score, teamwork rating, mentoring reputation, reporting and recognition systems.
7. Leaderboards
Global, regional, friends-only, guild, seasonal and private leaderboards with configurable visibility.
8. Communication
Chat, voice notes, announcements, AI-assisted moderation, event scheduling and invitations.
9. Safety & Moderation
Privacy controls, blocking, reporting, parental options, anti-spam and community guidelines.
10. Decision Rationale
Why collaborative progression outperforms isolated habit tracking; alternatives and trade-offs.
11. Edge Cases
Guild abandonment, inactive leaders, harassment, fake accounts, cheating, timezone differences.
12. Example Scenarios
Student study guild, entrepreneur mastermind, fitness challenge team, travel club.
13. Implementation Notes
Social graph, notification service, moderation tools, synchronization, scalability.
14. Risks
Community toxicity, privacy concerns, notification overload, unfair rankings.
15. Future Enhancements
Cross-platform guilds, live events, tournaments, community marketplaces and AI moderators.
