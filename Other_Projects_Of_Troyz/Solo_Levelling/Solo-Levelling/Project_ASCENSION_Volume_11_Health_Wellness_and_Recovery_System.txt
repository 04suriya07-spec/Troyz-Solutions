PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 11 – Health, Wellness & Recovery SystemBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define the health systems that connect physical and mental wellbeing to player progression. This volume covers recovery, fitness, sleep, nutrition, stress, energy, wellness analytics, and health-focused gameplay.
1. Executive Summary
Overview of the Health System and its role in sustainable long-term progression.
2. Design Goals
Promote healthy habits without encouraging unsafe behavior or replacing professional medical advice.
3. Health Architecture
Sleep, hydration, nutrition, exercise, recovery, stress, energy, mobility, mindfulness and wellness metrics.
4. Fitness System
Workouts, cardio, strength, flexibility, endurance, step tracking, recovery days and adaptive plans.
5. Nutrition System
Meal logging (optional), hydration tracking, nutrition goals, healthy eating challenges.
6. Recovery Engine
Rest days, burnout detection, adaptive difficulty reduction, wellness recommendations.
7. Health Integrations
Wearables, Health Connect, step counters, sleep tracking and optional third-party integrations.
8. Wellness Rewards
Health XP, recovery bonuses, wellness titles, streaks and long-term achievements.
9. AI Health Coach
Personalized reminders, recovery advice, workout suggestions, stress management and habit recommendations.
10. Decision Rationale
Why sustainable health progression is prioritized over constant challenge; alternatives and trade-offs.
11. Edge Cases
Illness, injury, disability, missing sensor data, device changes, inconsistent tracking.
12. Example Scenarios
Student during exams, athlete training, office worker improving activity, recovery after illness.
13. Implementation Notes
Health data permissions, synchronization, privacy, analytics, battery considerations.
14. Risks
Privacy, inaccurate health data, overtraining encouragement, notification fatigue.
15. Future Enhancements
AI wellness plans, smart device ecosystem, advanced recovery analytics, preventive coaching.
