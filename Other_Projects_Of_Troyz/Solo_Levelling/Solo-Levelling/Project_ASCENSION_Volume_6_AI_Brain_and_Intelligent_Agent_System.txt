PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 6 – AI Brain & Intelligent Agent SystemBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define the AI architecture that powers personalization, decision-making, coaching, storytelling, and automation. This volume establishes the responsibilities of every AI agent, how they collaborate, and the safety rules governing them.
1. Executive Summary
Overview of the AI ecosystem and how multiple specialized agents work together instead of a single monolithic assistant.
2. Design Goals
Personalization, transparency, safety, privacy, adaptability, explainability, and reliability.
3. AI Agent Architecture
Quest Master, Behavior Analyst, Story Narrator, Health Coach, Finance Coach, Learning Coach, Guardian, Motivation Coach, Career Advisor, Travel Planner, Social Coach.
4. Memory System
Short-term memory, long-term preferences, contextual memory, player history, opt-in data retention.
5. Decision Engine
Context gathering, priority scoring, conflict resolution, recommendation ranking, confidence scoring.
6. Personalization Engine
Learning habits, preferred challenge level, schedules, interests, accessibility needs, adaptive coaching.
7. Conversation System
Voice, text, notifications, proactive suggestions, emotional tone selection, multi-language support.
8. AI Collaboration
How agents share information, delegate tasks, avoid conflicts, and maintain consistent player experience.
9. Safety & Guardrails
Privacy boundaries, consent, refusal rules, hallucination handling, sensitive-topic safeguards, user override.
10. Decision Rationale
Why multiple specialized agents are preferred over one general-purpose AI; alternatives and trade-offs.
11. Edge Cases
No internet, contradictory user goals, incomplete data, AI disagreement, corrupted memory, permission denial.
12. Example Scenarios
Morning planning, burnout detection, travel planning, exam preparation, financial coaching.
13. Implementation Notes
Model orchestration, APIs, prompt templates, memory storage, latency targets, monitoring.
14. Risks
Over-automation, privacy concerns, incorrect recommendations, excessive notifications, model drift.
15. Future Enhancements
Local AI models, multimodal understanding, wearable integration, collaborative AI teams.
