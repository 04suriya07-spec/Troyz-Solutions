PROJECT ASCENSIONVolume 5 – Quest Engine & Mission GenerationBlueprint (Foundation)
Purpose: Define the Quest Engine that transforms real-world activities into dynamic, personalized missions. This volume specifies quest generation, categorization, rewards, AI adaptation, validation, and lifecycle management.
1. Executive Summary
Overview of the Quest Engine and its role in player progression.
2. Design Goals
Generate meaningful, achievable and personalized quests while preventing repetition and burnout.
3. Quest Categories
Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Seasonal, Story, Secret, Emergency, Health, Learning, Finance, Career, Social, Travel, Community and AI-generated quests.
4. Quest Lifecycle
Creation, assignment, acceptance, tracking, completion, failure, retry, archival.
5. AI Quest Generator
Inputs (calendar, goals, habits, weather, health, location, preferences), constraints and personalization rules.
6. Rewards & Penalties
XP, currencies, titles, items, reputation, narrative progression, balanced penalties.
7. Dynamic Difficulty
Adaptive quest complexity based on history, consistency, skill level and recovery needs.
8. Quest Validation
Manual completion, sensor verification, integrations, anti-cheat validation.
9. World & Story Integration
Quests trigger world events, NPC interactions, boss battles and story progression.
10. Decision Rationale
Why adaptive quests outperform static task lists; alternatives and trade-offs.
11. Edge Cases
Offline mode, impossible quests, missed deadlines, conflicting calendars, disabled permissions, duplicate tasks.
12. Example Scenarios
Student exam week, entrepreneur launch week, traveler, fitness challenge, recovery period.
13. Implementation Notes
Quest service, scheduling engine, event bus, notification system, AI orchestration.
14. Risks
Quest fatigue, unfair difficulty, excessive notifications, privacy considerations.
15. Future Enhancements
Community-created quests, marketplace, AI-designed campaigns, cross-platform events.
