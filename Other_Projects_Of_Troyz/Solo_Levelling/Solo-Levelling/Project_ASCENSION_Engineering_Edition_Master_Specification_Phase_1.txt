Recommended Development Order
Begin by fully expanding Volume 1 into an engineering-grade specification. Only after Volume 1 is complete should Volume 2 be expanded, because later volumes depend on definitions established in the earlier ones.
Project Scale
The complete Engineering Edition is expected to exceed 3,000 pages and should be developed iteratively. Each expanded volume will become a standalone technical manual for designers, developers, QA engineers, product managers, and AI engineers.
